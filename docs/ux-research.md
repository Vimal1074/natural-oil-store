# Commerce UX inspiration notes

**Reviewed:** 9 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Adapt useful customer experience patterns for the independent Natural Oil demonstration. These are references, not copied layouts, branding, or assets.

## Sites and patterns reviewed

- [BigBasket cold-pressed oils](https://www.bigbasket.com/pc/foodgrains-oil-masala/edible-oils-ghee/cold-pressed-oil/?nc=nb): listings surface pack size, price, offers, and ratings together. The storefront uses visible pack options, dated price context, and category filters.
- [Swiggy Instamart oils](https://www.swiggy.com/instamart/category-listing?categoryName=Oils+and+Ghee&custom_back=true): the category page groups oil types such as mustard, sunflower, and cold-pressed oils. This informed the simple oil-type filters.
- [Apple shopping experience](https://www.apple.com/shop/help/shopping_experience): customers can save products for later, continue shopping as guests, and follow order status after purchase. The demo adopts saved-for-later and visible order progression as prototypes.
- [Tata Neu](https://www.tata.com/business/tata-digital/tata-neu): the service combines category commerce with a rewards balance redeemable on future purchases. This informed the sample points and redemption interaction.
- [Baymard filter UX guidance](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-filter-ui): research recommends relevant filters, visible applied choices, and clear results; on mobile, a clear apply/show-results step can help. The demo keeps filtering simple for its small catalog and shows the active category and result count.

## Ideas applied to the prototype

1. Keep product finding quick with search, oil categories, sorting, and clear result counts.
2. Help compare choices using visible pack details, a save-for-later list, and a side-by-side comparison tray.
3. Support confidence and repeat use with a product guide, sample batch lookup, demo delivery estimate, subscriptions, points, and an order timeline.
4. Keep staff operations out of the customer journey with a separate `/employee` workspace for attendance, payroll preparation, and sample business analytics.

## Limits

These features are front-end demonstrations and do not connect to a courier, an AI provider, a rewards ledger, a payment service, or real employee records. The employee portal has no authentication and must never hold real HR or salary information in its current form.
